About

Hi, I’m Jen.

I’ve always been fascinated by what it means to be human: how we respond, how we change, and what becomes possible when we understand ourselves better.

Over more than 20 years working in sustainability and purpose-driven marketing and communications, I’ve lived in six cities across three countries, repeatedly building new lives, finding my feet in unfamiliar places and navigating different professional and cultural environments. Along the way, I’ve experienced multiple corporate restructures, career transitions and the challenge of finding new professional direction, all while raising a young son. These experiences have given me a first-hand appreciation of how demanding life can be, how unsettling change and uncertainty can feel, and how much we draw on ourselves to find our footing again.

When my old habits for managing stress stopped working, embodied practice gave me new ways to meet change, and more energy for life. Now I teach what I study, while still navigating life as a business professional.

My training and teaching draw on Pilates, yoga, meditation and the art of self-reflection.

I teach weekly yoga classes, have a small cohort of one-on-one clients, and I’ve helped teams and groups at the University of Canterbury, Rolleston College, Brooksfield Property Group and various wellness studios learn to tap into the intelligence of their body to better manage complex lives in a complex world.
